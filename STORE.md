## Title
Split or Steal: Millions

## Short description (one line)
Split or steal a pot of up to $5,000,000. Trust, bluff or betray.

## Description
A pot of money appears between you and another player: sometimes a few thousand dollars, sometimes a 1% MEGA JACKPOT of $5,000,000.

You have 40 seconds to talk with quick messages. Then you both choose in secret:

- Both split: you each take half.
- One steals: the thief takes everything, the other gets nothing.
- Both steal: nobody gets anything.

Your split rate is public, so everyone knows if you are honest… or if you are pretending to be. Build trust, bluff, or betray at the perfect moment.

Spend your millions on cars, speedboats, mega yachts, beachfront villas, a private island and a private jet, and show off your best asset to every opponent. Climb the leaderboard of the richest players.

Play online against real players, or create a private room and play with your friends using a 5-letter code. If nobody is online, an AI player takes the seat.

All money in the game is virtual.

## Controls
- Mouse or touch: tap a message to talk, then tap Split or Steal.
- Create a room and share the code to play with a friend.
